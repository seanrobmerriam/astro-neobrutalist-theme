This one isn't ready. Flipping `--color-ink` and `--color-paper` gets you a
dark _background_, not a dark _theme_ — the six accents were picked for
contrast against off-white, and several of them go muddy or lose their
punch against near-black without their own pass.

Draft, not published, until that pass happens. This post exists in the
content collection specifically to prove that a `draft: true` entry is
excluded from the blog listing, the RSS feed, and the sitemap in a
production build, while still being viewable in dev — which is the actual
behavior a real editorial workflow needs.
